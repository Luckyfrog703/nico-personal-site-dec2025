Bay Area Events Tracker

A "what's going on today?" tracker for the Bay Area — real, dated events from public listings, browsable by day, area, and category.

Problem

Finding out what's happening locally on a given day means either doom-scrolling five different sites or asking a chatbot — which hallucinates dates and invents events with total confidence. I kept doing the second one. Neither is a product.

Findings

The unit of the question is the day, not the event: "what's on Saturday?" is how people actually ask, so the UI leads with a date strip, not a search box. Event data is fragmented across Ticketmaster, Eventbrite, Funcheap, DoTheBay, and venue calendars — no single source is complete, so the product is aggregation plus dedupe, not a feed. Trust rules from the Rental Sifter apply doubly here: a wrong date is worse than a missing event. Every event must carry an explicit date and a verbatim source link, or it doesn't ship.

Decisions

  • Real public listings only, validated in code: explicit date required, source URL copied from the actual listing, duplicates merged (the same event listed on two sites collapses to one card, preferring the event-specific page).
  • Date strip (Today / Tomorrow / This weekend / any day), area chips (SF, Oakland & East Bay, San Jose & South Bay, Peninsula, North Bay), category chips (Music, Comedy, Theater & Arts, Sports, Festivals & Markets, Food & Drink, Free), plus search.
  • "My picks" saves survive after an event rolls off the listings — a plan shouldn't evaporate because a feed refreshed.
  • Refresh on demand, automatically when stale, and every morning at 7:30am.
  • Disclosed limitation: a few source links land on the section page an event was gathered from rather than its own page; the app never fabricates a URL and tells readers to confirm details at the source.

Outcome

A live app; refreshes pull 115–140 real dated events covering the next three weeks (verified examples: Sharks games at SAP Center, Fred Armisen at the Castro, Funcheap night markets, Eventbrite comedy with real prices). A duplicated listing and time-format inconsistencies were caught and fixed in verification.

Metrics

Date-strip usage by day offset (do people plan ahead or same-day?); picks per user; source click-through rate.

Roadmap

Version 2: favorite-artist alerts (starting with my own list — ATEEZ, 311, Provoker, Cannons) pushed into the morning refresh. Version 3: "plan my Saturday" bundles — an afternoon + evening pairing by area.

Interactive app — happy to demo live on request.
